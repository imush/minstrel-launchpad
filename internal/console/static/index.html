Outside access

How the app reaches home when you're away.

Forward a TCP port on your router to this launchpad (port ), then enter the address the outside world sees: a static IP or a dynamic DNS name, with the router's port. Port 443 gets through the most hotel and office networks. One per line.

Set by -public / LAUNCHPAD_PUBLIC; change it there.

Paired devices pick up a changed address the next time they connect.

To use it from outside, allow inbound TCP to port on the router — a firewall rule, not a redirect. It is the only way in when your provider shares one IPv4 address between homes.

No port to forward: the launchpad connects out to a minstrel-satellite, and the app goes through it when it can't reach home directly. Your music stays encrypted all the way; the satellite only passes it on. Paste the invitation link from the satellite's owner.
